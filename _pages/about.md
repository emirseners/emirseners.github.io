I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Industrial Engineering at Boğaziçi University, where I also earned my B.S. in the same field.

My research interests lie in mathematical optimization, with a particular focus on stochastic and convex optimization. I am especially interested in exploiting the underlying structure of optimization problems to develop tailored solution methods and algorithms. My research experience includes applications in energy systems and water distribution networks.
